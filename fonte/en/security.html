Security policy

How we protect your account and your data

What the PRONEX app does to keep Study and Freelancer safe. Last updated: 24 November 2026.

In short

  • The website is public and needs no account. Study and Freelancer only open after you sign in.
  • Everything travels over HTTPS. Accounts are handled by Google's Firebase Authentication; PRONEX never sees your password.
  • The database has rules that check who you are on every read and write: you can only change your own data.
  • If you find a flaw, tell us privately. We reply and fix it as fast as we can.

1. Where an account is needed

The PRONEX website (home, services, portfolio, price request and contact) is open to everyone and uses no cookies. The Study and Freelancer sections run inside the PRONEX app and only show content to signed-in users. There is no public demo mode: without an account, there is no access to data.

2. Accounts and passwords

  • You can sign in with email and password or with your Google account.
  • Passwords need at least 8 characters, with letters and numbers. They are stored and checked by Firebase Authentication, never by PRONEX.
  • With email sign-up, we ask you to confirm your address through a verification message.
  • If you forget your password, you get a reset link by email.
  • Before deleting your account, we ask you to confirm your identity again.

3. How data is protected

  • In transit: the website and the apps are only served over HTTPS (GitHub Pages), and the connection to the database is encrypted too.
  • In the database: Cloud Firestore enforces security rules on the server. Every request is checked against your session: you can only read what you are allowed to and only change your own records. The rules also limit the size and format of what is saved.
  • On your device: to work offline, the app keeps a copy of your data in the browser's storage. On a shared device, sign out when you are done.

4. How the app is protected

  • Freelancer has a content security policy (CSP) that only lets code run from PRONEX itself and the Google services needed to sign in and save data.
  • Text written by other people is treated as text, never as code.
  • Links to other websites do not pass on the full address of the page you were on.
  • In Freelancer you can block and report profiles.

5. What you can do

  • Use a password you don't use on other websites.
  • Sign out when you use someone else's or a school computer.
  • Be wary of messages asking for your password: PRONEX never asks for it.

6. Reporting a security flaw

If you find a vulnerability, write to pronex.protocol@gmail.com with the subject "Security" and explain how to reproduce it. Please don't disclose it or access other people's data while we fix it. We reply as fast as we can. The contact is also in security.txt.

7. Changes to this policy

If anything changes, we update this page and the date at the top. How we handle personal data is in the privacy policy.
