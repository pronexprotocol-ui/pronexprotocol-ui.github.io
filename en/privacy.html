Privacy policy

Your data, explained without small print.

What data we collect, why, and what you can do about it. Last updated: 3 October 2026.

In short

  • We only collect what you send us: forms, messages, emails or calls.
  • We use that data only to reply to you, book calls and prepare your proposal.
  • We don't use cookies, advertising or visitor counters.
  • We delete everything after 12 months if the project doesn't go ahead.
  • You can ask to see, correct or delete your data at any time.

1. Who is responsible for your data

PRONEX, represented by João Paiva, based in Lousã (Coimbra), Portugal. For anything about your data, email pronex.protocol@gmail.com or call +351 912 507 674.

2. What data we collect

Only what you send us yourself:

  • In the price request form: your name, a description of what you need and one way to contact you, which can be your email or, if you prefer WhatsApp or a call, your phone number (required); type of business, business name or Instagram @, a website address, how much you're thinking of investing, how you'd like us to reply and how you found us (optional); and your choices (website type, pages, extras, timing, care plan and estimated price).
  • In the booking calendar: name and email (required), phone number, the format, day and time you chose, the subject, any note you write and your device's time zone, so we get the time right.
  • By WhatsApp, phone or email: your number or email address and whatever you tell us or write to us.

We don't collect anything automatically while you browse: the site uses no cookies, advertising or statistics tools. The service that hosts the site records technical data about each visit (see section 5).

3. What we use the data for

  • Replying to your request and booking or confirming calls.
  • Preparing the proposal with the price and the deadline.
  • Talking to you during the project, if we go ahead.

We never use your data for advertising and never add you to mailing lists unless you ask.

4. Legal basis

Your consent, which you give by ticking the box in the last step of the form, and the fact that your request is the first step towards a possible contract (Article 6(1)(a) and (b) of the General Data Protection Regulation, GDPR).

5. Who else has access

We don't sell or share your data with anyone for commercial purposes. For the site and the forms to work, we use three external services:

  • FormSubmit (formsubmit.co): delivers the form message to our email.
  • Gmail (Google): where the request is stored.
  • Hosting (Netlify): stores the website files and records technical data about each visit, such as the IP address, for security. We don't look at those records to identify you.

If you talk to us on WhatsApp, the conversation also goes through Meta's services, under WhatsApp's own rules.

These services may process data outside the European Union.

6. How long we keep it

  • If we don't go ahead: we delete your request after 12 months.
  • If we go ahead: we keep the data during the project and, afterwards, only what the law requires us to keep for invoicing.

7. What is stored on your device

A few things are stored only in your browser, on this device, and are never sent to us:

  • A draft of the price request form, so you don't lose what you wrote if you close the page by accident. It's deleted when you send the request or click "Start again".
  • Your choice of light or dark mode, so the site remembers it next time.
  • Your choice to pause the animations, if you click "Pause animations" at the bottom of the page.
  • The language notice: if you close the notice saying the site also exists in another language, we store that choice so we don't show it again.

These are not advertising or statistics cookies: you can delete them in your browser settings at any time.

8. Your rights

At any time, and free of charge, you can:

  • find out what data we hold about you and ask for a copy;
  • correct wrong data;
  • ask us to delete your data;
  • withdraw the consent you gave.

Just email pronex.protocol@gmail.com. We reply within 30 days at most. If you think we haven't handled your data properly, you can complain to the Portuguese data protection authority, CNPD (cnpd.pt), or to the authority in your own country.

9. Changes to this policy

If anything changes, we update this page and the date at the top.
