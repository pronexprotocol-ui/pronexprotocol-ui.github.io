PRONEX Freelancer

Ask for help and show what you can do

Anyone who needs help posts a request and anyone who can do it replies. Finished work goes into the portfolio of the person who did it. Free, inside the PRONEX app. In Portuguese.

Open Freelancer → How to install

What's in Freelancer

  • Requests and offersPost what you need, receive offers and choose who to work with. The agreement and the deliveries are kept in the request's chat.
  • PortfolioEvery finished job goes into your profile. Good ratings confirm your skills.
  • Questions and study sessionsAsk and answer: the most useful answers rise to the top and teachers' answers are highlighted. Book sessions at school or online.
  • NetworkConnections, groups and openings posted by verified teachers.
  • Safe for schoolTeachers verified by the administrator, reporting and moderation. There are no payments inside the app.
Who it's for
Students and teachers aged 13 and over
Price
Free. There are no payments in the app

Install

Install the PRONEX app in three taps

No app store needed. Open Study or Freelancer and add it to your home screen: you get one app, “PRONEX”, with both sections.

Android

  1. Open the section in Chrome.
  2. Tap the ⋮ menu.
  3. Choose “Install app” or “Add to Home screen”.

iPhone

  1. Open the section in Safari.
  2. Tap Share (the square with the arrow).
  3. Choose “Add to Home Screen”.

Computer

  1. Open the section in Chrome or Edge.
  2. Click the install icon at the end of the address bar.
  3. Confirm with “Install”.
