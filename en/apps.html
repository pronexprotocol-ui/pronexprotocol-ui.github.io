Apps

PRONEX apps: study and freelancer

Two apps from the brand, for phone and computer. The apps are in Portuguese.

Everything in one app

The PRONEX app puts Study and Freelancer together, with a tab for each. Install it once and you get both (in Portuguese).

Open the PRONEX app →

Study

Pronex_Study

Study at the level of your goal: exercises with new numbers every time, formulas, a calculator and your progress.

  • Exercises by subject
  • Formulas and step-by-step help
  • Class, ranking and progress
Open Study → (opens the app, in Portuguese)

Freelancer

Pronex_Link

Ask for help from people who know, and show what you can do. For students and teachers, with a portfolio of finished work.

  • Job requests and offers
  • Portfolio and network
  • Questions and study sessions
Open Freelancer → (opens the app, in Portuguese)

One account for both apps: sign in to one and you are already in the other, on the same browser.

How to start

  1. Pick the app

    Study to practise and follow your progress; Freelancer to ask for help or show what you can do.

  2. Sign in

    The same account works in both apps. Pronex_Link also has a demo you can try without an account.

  3. Add it to your home screen

    On your phone, open the browser menu and choose “Add to Home screen”. The app opens like any other, even offline.
