PRONEX Study

Revise for tests without memorising answers

Exercises with new numbers every time, flashcards, exam mode and the Nex tutor, in 10 secondary-school subjects of the Portuguese curriculum. Free, inside the PRONEX app. In Portuguese.

Open Study → How to install

What's in Study

  • Always new exercisesThe numbers change on every attempt. When you get one wrong, you see the step-by-step solution and where the calculation went off track.
  • FlashcardsFormulas and ideas you miss come back the next day. The ones you know come back later, so you do not forget them.
  • Exam mode and study planTimed questions with no hints, like the real test. The plan spreads the material over the days you have left.
  • Nex tutorExplains things another way, checks your reasoning and creates exercises. It runs on your device: your questions are not sent to any company.
  • Study with your classA questions wall, study groups, one-on-one duels and a weekly ranking with leagues.
Who it's for
Secondary-school students aged 13 and over

Install

Install the PRONEX app in three taps

No app store needed. Open Study or Freelancer and add it to your home screen: you get one app, “PRONEX”, with both sections.

Android

  1. Open the section in Chrome.
  2. Tap the ⋮ menu.
  3. Choose “Install app” or “Add to Home screen”.

iPhone

  1. Open the section in Safari.
  2. Tap Share (the square with the arrow).
  3. Choose “Add to Home Screen”.

Computer

  1. Open the section in Chrome or Edge.
  2. Click the install icon at the end of the address bar.
  3. Confirm with “Install”.
