About PRONEX · web design in Lousã, Portugal

Who makes your websites: a new studio, with nothing to hide.

We're not an agency with 10 years behind it and 20 awards. We're a new studio that shows everything: prices, deadlines and how we work. And we treat every project as the most important one in our portfolio, because it is.

Who's behind it

João Paiva, aka Kaizer.

PRONEX was born in Lousã, a small town in central Portugal, out of frustration: seeing creators with excellent work and an online image that didn't do it justice. I started by making websites for people close to me: a photographer, a bakery in town. I realised the problem was almost always the same. Talent wasn't missing. What was missing was someone to do agency-quality work at a price people could afford.

Today PRONEX makes websites, brands and 3D animations. Every website is designed and built from scratch, without the ready-made templates of platforms like Wix or WordPress.

I work to short deadlines and take on only a few projects at a time. I'd rather say "I can't fit it in this week" than deliver something ordinary. And I work in Portuguese and English, with clients in Portugal and abroad.

How we work

Six rules we don't bend.

  • Listen first, design second

    Before we start, we find out who your clients are and what you want them to do on your site.

  • Prices in plain sight

    They're on the website. The form works out the price before we even talk.

  • Made from scratch

    No heavy templates. Light, fast websites where we know the reason behind every detail. This page, for example, weighs at most 218 KB, with all its images.

  • Phone first

    Most of your visitors come from Instagram, on their phone. We design for them first.

  • Accessible to everyone

    Easy to read and use for everyone, including people with low vision or who browse with a keyboard only.

  • With a user guide

    We hand over a guide so you can use your site without depending on us.

Transparency

What we don't do.

Better to know now than halfway through the project.

  • Online shops built from scratch (we recommend Shopify)
  • Rush jobs without agreeing the extra cost first
  • Promises of more followers or more sales
  • Generic photos taken from the internet
  • Copies of other people's websites
  • Charging for changes that were already included in the price
