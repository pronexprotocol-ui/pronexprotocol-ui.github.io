Process

Five steps. No surprises.

You always know what stage the project is at, what comes next and when.

  1. To start

    Chat

    You fill in the form and we reply within 24 hours (working days) to arrange a 30-minute chat about your business and what you want from the site.

  2. up to 48h later

    Proposal

    You get the fixed price, the delivery date and the list of what's included. We start once you pay the first half.

  3. 1st half of the schedule

    Design

    We show you how the site will look, on computer and phone. With Brand + Website, this stage starts with the 3 logo options. You can ask for changes twice.

  4. 2nd half of the schedule

    Build

    We build the real site, with animations and the form. You follow the progress on a private link. Here too, you can ask for changes twice.

  5. Delivery

    Site live

    We put the site live on your address and hand over the user guide. You get 30 days of free help.

Example for a Business Website (2–4 weeks). With a Digital Card, all of this fits into 3–5 days.

Frequently asked questions

Before you ask.

How does payment work?

You pay half when you accept the proposal, to book your slot, and the other half on delivery, before the site goes live. Prices and payment are in euros: we accept bank transfer to our IBAN (SEPA transfers within Europe) and, if you have a Portuguese bank account, MB WAY. Above €800 you can pay in three instalments.

Will the website be in my name?

Yes. The website address (for example, yourname.com) is registered in your name and you receive all the files and passwords. If you ever want to work with someone else, you take everything with you.

What if I don't like the design?

You get 2 rounds of changes at each stage (design and build), at no extra cost. We show you how the site will look before we build it, so the big changes happen early, when they're easy to make. From the third round on, each one costs €25.

Can I update the website myself later?

Simple changes, like a price, your opening hours or a sentence, yes: the user guide explains it step by step. For anything else you have three options: pay by the hour (€25), the Essential plan (€25/month) or the Grow plan (€45/month). See the care plans. If you're going to post news every week, choose the "News section" extra, which has an easy dashboard.

Are there any costs after the website is finished?

Just one: the website address (for example, yourname.com), which costs between €10 and €25 a year and which you pay directly to the company that registers it. The space where the site is stored online is free, because our websites are light. We take care of the set-up. Maintenance is optional. Guide (in Portuguese): how much a website costs in Portugal.

Do you build online shops?

We don't build online shops from scratch yet. To sell products we recommend Shopify, a platform made for shops, and we take care of the shop's look and brand. We'd rather tell you this before you pay us, not after.

I have a small budget. Is there an option?

Yes. The Digital Card starts at €150 and already gives you a professional presence: one screen with what you do, contact details, opening hours and map. Later you can move to a bigger site and we reuse the work already done. Above €800 you can also pay in three instalments.

Can I talk to someone before asking for a price?

Of course. Message us on WhatsApp, call +351 912 507 674 (Monday to Friday, 18:00–21:00; Saturday, 10:00–13:00, Lisbon time) or book a free 15-minute call on the day and time that suit you.

Can my website be in two languages?

Yes. A second language costs €120 extra (Portuguese and English). Ideal for holiday rentals, tourism and businesses with international clients. This site also has a Portuguese version.

Do you work with clients outside Portugal?

Yes. We're based in Lousã, Portugal, but everything can be done remotely, in English: we talk by video call, phone or WhatsApp and you watch the site take shape on a private link. Calls are booked in Lisbon time; tell us your time zone and we'll find a slot that works. If you're near Lousã or Coimbra, we can also meet in person.
